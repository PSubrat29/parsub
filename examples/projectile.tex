\section{Projectile motion}

We aim to compute the trajectory of a projectile under gravity, where the
gravitational acceleration is $g = 9.81$.

The equation of motion is given by:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:trajectory}
y = x \tan(\theta) - \frac{g x^2}{2 v_0^2 \cos^2(\theta)}
\end{equation}
where $y$ is the height, $x$ the horizontal distance, $\theta$ the launch
angle and $v_0$ the initial velocity.

We want to find the maximum range. The range equation is:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:range}
R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}
\end{equation}

The time of flight is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:time}
T = \frac{2 v_0 \sin(\theta)}{g}.
\end{equation}
